# Embodiment Game – Print Setup Guide

This guide outlines how to prepare all physical components required to run the Embodiment Game. It includes instructions for printing cards, reflection sheets, and optional fabrication of engraved dice.

---

## 🎲 Dice (Optional Fabrication)

**File:** `EmbodimentGame_DiceIcons.svg` or `EmbodimentGame_DiceIcons.pdf`  
**Purpose:** Laser engraving custom **D12 dice** to assign:
- Character Archetypes
- Personality Traits
- Impairments

**Instructions:**
- Create a jig that fits your laser engraving machine and holds each D12 securely.
- Use wooden or acrylic dice blanks.
- Icons are sized for one face of a standard D12.
- You may engrave one die per trait set (3 dice per group) or combine traits on a single die if preferred.

> 📝 Standard numbered D12s can be used instead with a trait reference sheet if engraving is not feasible.

---

## 🃏 Cards – Roles and Wildcards

**File:** `EmbodimentGame_Cards_PrintSet.pdf`  
**Paper Type:** Avery C32010-25 Business Card Sheets  
**Card Size:** 85 x 54 mm (standard credit card size)  
**Settings:**
- **Double-sided (duplex)** printing enabled
- Print **at 100% scale** (disable "fit to page")
- **Flip on long edge**
- Use **colour printing** for clarity and legibility

**Instructions:**
1. Load Avery C32010-25 paper into your printer.
2. Print using duplex settings.
3. After printing, tear along the perforations to separate the cards.

> 🔁 If you do not have Avery stock, print on cardstock and cut manually to 85 × 54 mm.

---

## 📄 Reflection Sheets

**File:** `EmbodimentGame_ReflectionSheets.pdf`  
**Paper Size:** A4 or US Letter  
**Settings:**  
- Single-sided  
- Black and white printing is sufficient

**Use:**  
Each participant will need approximately 3 reflection sheets to complete all rounds. Print multiple copies depending on group size.
