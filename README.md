# The Embodiment Game

The Embodiment Game is a role-playing design tool that helps students, educators, and practitioners explore how users interact with products in real-world contexts. Through structured gameplay, participants rotate through the roles of **User**, **Device**, and **Designer** to simulate and reflect on accessibility challenges, unintended consequences, and emotional responses in product interaction.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This game supports the development of **empathy**, **critical reflection**, and **human-centred design skills**. It is particularly suited for:

- Product and UX design students
- Educators teaching inclusive and accessible design
- Facilitators of design thinking workshops
- Anyone exploring user-product interactions

## 🕒 Duration & Setup

- **Time required:** 60–90 minutes
- **Players:** 3+ (in groups of 3)
- **Materials Needed:**
  - Printed cards (or digital display)
  - Dice (3 per group)
  - Reflection sheets or notebooks
  - Space for movement and observation

---

## 🧩 Game Structure

Each group plays three rounds, rotating roles each time:

1. **Round 1** – Core interaction setup
2. **Round 2** – Introduce wildcard challenges (emotional, environmental, or device-based)
3. **Round 3** – Combine multiple modifiers and push for creative resolution

After each round, players complete a **Quick Reflection**, followed by a **Structured Group Reflection** at the end.

---

## 🧠 Learning Outcomes

- Explore diverse user perspectives and accessibility needs
- Identify breakdowns between intended and actual interaction
- Reflect on the emotional and contextual dimensions of design
- Translate insights into actionable design improvements
